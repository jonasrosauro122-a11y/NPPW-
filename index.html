Current business pain points translated into a support system

The system below is based on NPPW's recurring service model for national retail locations.

Pain Point 01

ServiceChannel verification overload

Team members spend large blocks of time checking subcontractor compliance: check-in/out, time on site, ticket notes, and photos.

Pain Point 02

Acuity to Jobber re-entry

Delivery requests enter through Acuity, but they must still be re-entered in Jobber for scheduling and invoicing preparation, increasing labor and overbooking risk.

Pain Point 03

Leadership visibility gap

Operations leadership needs a clean daily and weekly summary of work order status, vendor compliance, and scheduling exceptions.

ServiceChannel verification desk model

The VA becomes a structured verification desk, not just a data entry assistant.

Verification checklist flow

1. Open ticket and confirm site identityVerify store number, location, service date, and work order type.
2. Review check-in and check-outConfirm the subcontractor recorded presence in the system correctly.
3. Compare time on site to service standardExample: flag a 30-minute visit if the work usually requires 1–1.5 hours.
4. Validate photo complianceCheck for before/after images, quality, relevance, and upload completeness.
5. Review notes and exceptionsConfirm any issue notes, weather notes, or service completion updates are documented.
6. Escalate if non-compliantIf check-in, timing, or proof is missing, the VA logs the exception and escalates using the standard protocol.
7. Record final statusCompleted, pending clarification, rejected proof, or needs call-back to ServiceChannel.

Role ownership inside the operating system

Each tool has a specific purpose to prevent overlap and confusion.

Acuity

Booking intake for delivery-related scheduling. It is the front-door trigger, not the final operating source of truth.

Zapier

Automation bridge used to move standardized data from one platform to another and reduce repetitive re-entry.

Jobber

Operational job / scheduling and invoicing-prep environment for downstream work coordination.

Google Sheets

Leadership-facing reporting log for exceptions, completion status, trends, and operational transparency.

Leadership Takeaway

Why this demo matters

Automation helps move clean data faster, but it does not replace operational judgment. The VA handles validation, exception review, ServiceChannel proof checking, and communication follow-up. That is the real value proposition for NPPW.

Recommended starting scope

Start with work order verification, ticket updates, and Acuity → Jobber support. Once stable, expand into reporting assistance, web updates, and marketing / SEO admin support.

Recommended rollout phases

A practical 5-phase implementation path for the CEO to review.

Phase 1

Process capture

Document exact verification rules, escalation paths, and the Acuity-to-Jobber fields required by operations.

Phase 2

VA training

Train one dedicated VA on ServiceChannel review, call handling, ticket management, and multi-system data flow.

Phase 3

Pilot support

Assign a defined location group or specialist pair and track turnaround time, issue rate, and compliance accuracy.

Phase 4

Automation hardening

Refine Zapier mapping rules, standardize error handling, and tighten reporting in Google Sheets.

Phase 5

Scale support

Expand into broader admin support, reporting, web updates, and basic SEO / marketing support.
