Core training modules

Each module below reflects the operational needs you provided for NPPW.

1. Attention to Detail & Accuracy Training

This is the critical foundation module and should be mastered first.

  • Data accuracy drills and error spotting exercises
  • Before/after validation practice
  • Double-check protocol system
  • Real work order review simulations
2. ServiceChannel Work Order Management

Teaches the VA to understand work order flow from creation to closure.

  • Work order lifecycle: create → dispatch → complete → close
  • Vendor assignment logic and position 1 vendor awareness
  • Ticket updates and documentation standards
  • Photo and timestamp verification rules
  • Handling exceptions: missing check-in, incomplete job, missing photos
3. Data Transfer & Multi-System Operations

Focuses on moving information across tools without causing duplicates or confusion.

  • Cross-system data mapping
  • Preventing duplicate entries
  • SOP-based data transfer flow
  • System role clarity: what belongs in each platform
4. Vendor Verification & Compliance

Builds the VA's ability to review proof of work and identify red flags.

  • Valid vs. invalid job completion
  • Time-on-site validation rules
  • Photo compliance review
  • Red flag identification
  • Escalation workflow for non-compliance
5. Communication & Phone Operations

Prepares the VA to handle calls professionally and document outcomes correctly.

  • Professional call handling scripts
  • Ticket update communication format
  • Vendor communication standards
  • Escalation language and protocols
  • Logging phone updates into systems
6. Checklist & SOP Discipline

Ensures the VA follows the process consistently every time.

  • Step-by-step checklist execution
  • Task completion verification method
  • No-step-skipping discipline system
  • Standard operating workflow practice
7. System Ecosystem Awareness

Teaches the VA how the systems connect and where each one begins and ends.

  • ServiceChannel for operations verification
  • Jobber for delivery / invoicing prep
  • Acuity booking limitations and double-booking risk
  • Internal tools and process boundaries
8. Reporting & Documentation Training

Provides visibility for leadership and supports issue tracking.

  • Daily and weekly reporting structure
  • Issue tracking logs
  • Performance summary creation
  • Escalation documentation standards
9. Marketing & Branding Support

Prepares for future support beyond operations once the admin workload is stabilized.

  • Basic SEO principles
  • Google Business Profile updates
  • Service description consistency
  • Simple content updates
  • Brand tone alignment

15-day training roadmap

A practical schedule that moves from foundations to live-process readiness.

Week 1 • Foundation & system understanding

Introduce the business, the service model, and the importance of precision in recurring work order operations.

Day 1NPPW business model, services, national retail context, and the VA role overview.
Day 2Attention to detail training, error spotting drills, and double-check protocol system.
Day 3System ecosystem overview: ServiceChannel, Acuity, Jobber, Google Sheets, internal tools.
Day 4Work order lifecycle and ticket documentation standards.
Day 5Checklist and SOP discipline workshop with review exercises.

Week 2 • Operations & compliance practice

Build confidence through real-world work order practice and exception handling drills.

Day 6ServiceChannel work order management and vendor assignment logic.
Day 7Photo, timestamp, and completion proof verification rules.
Day 8Vendor compliance training: valid vs invalid completion, red flags, time-on-site review.
Day 9Escalation handling for missing check-ins, incomplete jobs, or insufficient proof.
Day 10Phone handling, vendor communication, and logging call outcomes correctly.

Week 3 • Data flow, reporting & controlled handoff

Focus on system-to-system coordination, reporting clarity, and demonstration of readiness.

Day 11Cross-system data mapping and Acuity → Jobber process flow with duplicate prevention.
Day 12Google Sheets reporting, issue tracking logs, and weekly summary structure.
Day 13Simulated end-to-end scenarios covering booking, verification, escalation, and reporting.
Day 14Shadowing / supervised practice using a real or sample queue.
Day 15Final assessment, QA calibration, readiness sign-off, and roadmap for future marketing / branding support.

Assessment and control framework

How leadership can evaluate if the training worked.

Practical assessments

  • Accuracy drill score on work order review simulations
  • Checklist compliance during live practice
  • ServiceChannel verification accuracy
  • Phone script adherence and documentation quality
  • Correct data mapping from Acuity to Jobber
  • Daily reporting completion and clarity

QA expectations

  • No step skipping on critical workflows
  • Consistent escalation when proof is incomplete or questionable
  • Clear note-taking and issue classification
  • Protection against duplicate entries and bad data movement
  • Professional communication with internal team and vendors
  • Ability to summarize issues for leadership review

Leadership KPIs to track after go-live

Recommended measurement points for the CEO / management team.

Verification turnaround

Average time needed to review and clear each ticket or location batch.

Exception rate

How many tickets are flagged for missing check-in, low time on site, or missing photos.

Data accuracy

Percent of correctly transferred bookings / jobs without rework or duplication.

Reporting consistency

Whether daily and weekly logs are completed, useful, and on time for leadership review.
